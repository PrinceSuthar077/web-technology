COMMON QUESTIONS

StudentHub FAQ

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal that provides access to campus events, student information, profiles and support services.

How can I create a StudentHub account?

Click the Register option in the navigation menu and enter your student information to create an account.

How can I login to StudentHub?

Click the Login button and enter your registered email address and password.

How can I register for an event?

Open the Events page, select the event you want to attend and click the Register button.

Can I update my student profile?

Yes. Open your Profile page from the Dashboard to view and update your student information.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery option on the Login page or contact StudentHub support.

How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page and send a message using the contact form.

Can I provide feedback?

Yes. Visit the Feedback page to submit suggestions, comments or report an issue.
